# 03 — Business Model

## Core principle: free for CEOs, paid for by partners who want to serve them

Members pay nothing, permanently. The asset we build is **a trusted, verified, engaged audience of decision-makers**. That audience is extremely valuable to:
- private banks and wealth managers;
- law firms, auditors and consultancies;
- free zones and investment promotion agencies;
- VCs, PE firms and family offices;
- executive search firms;
- premium brands such as cars, aviation, hospitality and real estate.

**The member is never the product.** These rules are written into the Terms and the partner contracts:
1. **No sale or rental of member data**, ever. Partners never get contact lists.
2. **Partners reach members only through hosted experiences or member-initiated requests.**
3. **Every partner touchpoint is labelled** ("Hosted with ___") and is opt-in.
4. **Category exclusivity is capped**, so no single bank "owns" the network.
5. **Members can mute all partner content** and still keep every core feature.

## Revenue streams

| # | Stream | Who pays | What they get | Indicative pricing (AED, excl. 5% VAT) | Starts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founding / City Partners** | 4–6 anchors per city (bank, law firm, free zone, premium brand) | Category-exclusive partner title for a city, co-hosting of a set number of flagship events, brand presence in the app, partner insight briefings | 300k–600k / year per partner | Launch |
| 2 | **Event sponsorship** | Brands and firms | Host or co-host a dinner, roundtable or breakfast (20–40 CEOs). Sponsor covers venue and catering plus a fee; one partner speaker slot of 10 minutes or less | 40k–120k per event; flagship summit packages 150k–750k | Launch |
| 3 | **Trusted Advisor marketplace** | Law, accounting, banking, HR, tech and consulting firms | A vetted listing members can search, member-initiated proposal requests, a badge, and optional participation in topic Circles as experts | 15k–60k / year by tier and category | Month 9 |
| 4 | **Capital Partner seats** | VCs, PE, family offices, venture debt | Verified investor seat: discover members who opt in as "raising", send intro requests (member accepts or declines), host investor office hours | 40k–100k / year per seat | Month 9 |
| 5 | **Board & executive placement** | Companies, PE portfolio companies, via **licensed** search partners | Access to members who are "open to board/NED roles"; success-fee share | Rev-share of placement fee (typically 20–30% of first-year comp) | Month 12 |
| 6 | **CEO Insight reports** | Consultancies, banks, governments | Co-branded quarterly "CEO Pulse" built from **anonymised, aggregated** survey data (minimum cell size 20; no raw data) | 150k–400k per report sponsor | Month 12 |
| 7 | **City launch partnerships** | Government and economic development bodies in target cities | We launch a chapter that attracts and connects CEOs in their city; they fund launch costs and anchor events | 500k–2M per city launch | Year 2 |

**Optional later streams (only if they don't conflict with the "free" promise):**
- **Corporate Leadership Pass.** Large groups pay to give their *non-CEO* executives a separate "Next CEO" track. CEO membership itself stays free.
- **Concierge travel and experiences** through affiliate partners such as private aviation and hotels, with commission and clear disclosure.
- **Sponsored learning programmes**, for example "AI for CEOs" bootcamps funded by a tech partner.

**Deliberately excluded:** display ads, selling leads, pay-to-message, paid "boosts", and a paid tier for CEOs. Any of these would break trust and the free promise.

## Why partners will pay
- **Comparable spend:** banks and law firms already spend heavily on CEO dinners in Dubai, and a single well-run 30-CEO dinner is often valued at well into six figures in AED. We give them **verified attendance**, **better curation** and **measurement** (attendance, follow-up meetings requested) at similar or lower cost.
- **Measurable outcomes:** partner dashboards report anonymised aggregate reach, event attendance, advisor proposal requests and satisfaction scores. They never contain personal data without member consent.

## Funding need
- **Pre-seed / seed:** **USD 2.0–2.5M (≈ AED 7.5–9M)** gives an 18-month runway through Dubai launch and Abu Dhabi + Riyadh expansion.
- **Strategic angels:** founding CEO members themselves. This aligns incentives and seeds the network.
- **Non-dilutive:** apply to Dubai programmes such as in5, DIFC FinTech Hive / Innovation Hub and Hub71 (Abu Dhabi). Use city partnership fees for expansion.
- **Series A trigger:** about 2,500 members, at least 3 cities live, at least 70% partner renewal rate, and at least 40% of members active each month.

## Unit economics to watch
- **Cost to acquire a verified member:** target **< AED 400**, mostly from invitations and events rather than paid ads.
- **Partner renewal rate:** target **≥ 70%**.
- **Revenue per member per year:** target **≥ AED 5k** by year 3.
- **Event gross margin:** sponsor fee minus direct cost, target **≥ 40%**.
